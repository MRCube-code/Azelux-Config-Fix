1 Upload the QR screenshot

Privacy: QR reading happens in your browser. The screenshot and decoded config are not sent to an AI or uploaded by this page.

2 Check the decoded config

Nothing decoded yet. Keep the full payload, including the final @@MRxCubic@@acx marker.

3 Make a clearer QR for the original Azelux site

This regenerates a sharp, high-resolution QR from the exact decoded payload. It does not rewrite or simplify the config string.

Upload the downloaded PNG to the original Azelux page that reads QR codes. If that site expects a different payload format, a sharper QR alone cannot change the format.

4 Apply it in Minecraft

  1. Tap Download Config Pack and save the .mcpack file.
  2. Open the downloaded pack with Minecraft and let it import.
  3. Activate the pack as instructed by Azelux, then return to the client.

If Minecraft imports the pack but Azelux does not apply the config, the site's pack metadata must match the exact format expected by your client build.
